Open-source, citation-or-abstain Q&A over UAE health regulation (DHA, DoH, MOHAP) for clinics, compliance teams and healthtech entering the UAE. Version- and jurisdiction-aware, with exact PDF highlights and a Redis + Postgres semantic answer cache.
