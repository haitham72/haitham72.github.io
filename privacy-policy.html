Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers small personal projects and prototype apps built by Haitham Mohamed (including Instagram/Meta API integrations connected to this account). These are individual, non-commercial projects, not a company or public product.

What's collected

Where a project connects to a third-party platform (such as the Instagram API), it only processes the minimum data needed for that project to function — for example, message content and identifiers involved in a direct interaction with the app, used solely to generate a reply within that same interaction.

What's not done

  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data is used to train models or for any purpose beyond the immediate feature it supports.
  • No public-facing accounts are targeted — these projects interact only with accounts that have explicitly opted in as testers/collaborators.

Retention

Data handled by these projects is kept only as long as needed for the feature to work and is deleted on a short rolling basis. No long-term profile or history is retained.

Contact

Questions about a specific project or a request to remove data: reach out via the contact details on the main site.
